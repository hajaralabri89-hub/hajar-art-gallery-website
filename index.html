Midnight Bloom

A quiet meditation on light and stillness featuring a solitary tree spreading its branches against a deep blue night sky, illuminated by clusters of glowing yellow dots.

✨ Artwork Details

Size:50 × 50 cm
Medium:Acrylic on stretched cotton canvas (350 g/m²)
Technique:Dot painting (35 mm deep edge)
Year:2026

Story: A quiet meditation on light and stillness. Set against a deep blue night sky, a solitary tree spreads its branches outward in a balanced, open form. Clusters of yellow dots gather like small points of light among the branches, while scattered white marks suggest stars suspended in the surrounding space.

Composition: Simple and centred, allowing the contrast between darkness and illumination to carry the mood. The tree stands as a calm presence, rooted and steady, with the glowing canopy adding a sense of warmth and quiet wonder.

Whispers of the Shoreline

A minimalist, rhythmic seascape capturing the peaceful dialogue between land and sea through clean horizontal bands of warm earth tones and cool oceanic gradients.

✨ Artwork Details

Size:26 × 31 cm
Medium:Acrylic on canvas, framed
Technique:Dot painting
Year:2026

Story: The composition is built on clean, horizontal bands of color, moving from a warm, sandy foreground up through layered shades of deep teal and ocean blue, culminating in a dark midnight horizon. A scattering of delicate white dots drifts across the center of the water, mimicking the gentle sparkle of starlight or foam catching the night air.

Composition: The contrasting white line of the surf breaks up the deep blues, adding a soft texture and a sense of quiet motion to an otherwise still and structured composition. With its balance of warm earth tones and cool oceanic gradients, the piece invites the viewer to appreciate the calm, repetitive beauty of the tides and the serene vastness of the coast at dusk.

Quiet Current

An exploration of texture, rhythm, and subtle movement within a seemingly simple surface — inspired by the shifting shades of blue, the light on the water, and the calm rhythm of the Adriatic Sea in Split.

✨ Artwork Details

Size:80 × 60 × 3.5 cm (H × W × D)
Medium:Acrylic on canvas
Presentation:Unframed
Year:2026
Created by:Hajar Art Gallery

Story: Thousands of small dots build the surface, creating a field of deep turquoise that shifts gently as the eye moves across it. Scattered touches of pale pink, warm yellow, and soft green appear within the pattern, suggesting small traces of light across the water. Rather than depicting the Adriatic Sea literally, the work reflects the colours, atmosphere, and quiet feeling experienced there.

Composition: The repetition of the dots gives the painting a sense of patience and continuity, while the small variations keep the surface from feeling completely uniform. The calm rhythm of the sea became a starting point — and the painting carries that feeling without trying to copy it.
